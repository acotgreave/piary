The Case of the Five Missing Days

Piary began on 14 November 2020. Six years on, the group writes each entry five digits behind pi. This inquiry establishes when the days went missing, and why.

Findings

  • No days were skipped. Every loss came from writing to the same digit twice.
  • The losses happened in four incidents between January and November 2021. The gap has held at exactly five days since.
  • In each incident, nobody announced the day's digit, so the group followed whoever posted first.
  • The original Tableau viz kept the true count throughout. It was checked once, on 19 November 2021, and its answer was not acted on.

Contributing factors

  • No digit announced. Andy normally posts each day's digit with a song clue. In every incident that announcement was missing, late, or confused.
  • Late-night posting. Most entries arrive near midnight or the next morning, and the first entry of the night set the digit for everyone.
  • Pi's own patterns. Runs like 1 4 1, 1 1 and 3 3 make a repeated digit look correct.
  • Two counts, never reconciled. By November 2021 the group's count and the Tableau viz disagreed by four days, and nobody resolved it.

Recommendation

The group should choose one count and keep this site as the single reference. It can stay five digits behind and keep the history unbroken, or jump forward five digits to rejoin the true sequence. Either way, the daily digit should come from the site rather than from memory.

Method: the digit for each day was taken from the number of lines in that day's entries (single-word entries count as 1; days without text count as 0). That sequence was then matched against pi.
